# Quickstart: Diet Tracking App

## Prerequisites

- Node.js 20 or newer
- Expo CLI or Expo Go on a mobile device
- An iOS or Android simulator, or a physical device with Expo Go

## Setup

1. Install dependencies for the Expo app.
2. Start the development server.
3. Open the app in Expo Go or a simulator.

## Validation Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Add an ingredient

1. Open the app and navigate to the ingredient screen.
2. Add an ingredient with a name and nutrition values per unit.
3. Confirm the ingredient appears in the saved ingredient list.

### Scenario 2: Create a recipe

1. Open the recipe creation flow.
2. Select one or more saved ingredients and assign units used.
3. Save the recipe and confirm the total calories, protein, and fiber are displayed.

### Scenario 3: Log a day entry

1. Open the daily logging flow for a selected day.
2. Choose a recipe and select a portion factor such as 1/2.
3. Save the entry and verify the daily totals update.

### Scenario 4: Review targets and history

1. Set daily calorie, protein, and fiber targets.
2. Review the daily summary and confirm the remaining values are shown with a visual status.
3. Open history and confirm the app shows data for multiple days such as today and yesterday.
